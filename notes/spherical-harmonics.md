# Spherical harmonics before Legendre formulas

Do not begin with associated Legendre polynomials.

Begin with the sphere, the action of rotations on functions on the sphere, and the rotation-invariant Laplacian.

The question is:

> how does the space of angular functions decompose under rotations?

The answer is into finite-dimensional irreducible rotation representations. Each such piece is also an eigenspace of the spherical Laplacian.

That finite-dimensional space is the primary object.

## Basis comes later

After choosing a particular rotation axis, one may choose a basis adapted to rotations about that axis. Standard physics notation attaches integer labels to those basis elements.

Those labels are bookkeeping. They should not organize the explanation.

Only after choosing spherical coordinates do the basis functions acquire their familiar coordinate form involving associated Legendre functions and an azimuthal complex phase.

So the explanatory order is:

1. rotation action;
2. irreducible representation;
3. spherical-harmonic space;
4. optional choice of axis and basis;
5. coordinate expression;
6. associated Legendre function.

The decomposition exists before the coordinates do.
