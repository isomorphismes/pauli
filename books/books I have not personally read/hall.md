# Brian C. Hall — *Quantum Theory for Mathematicians*

Suggested because it treats quantum mechanics with substantial attention to Lie groups, Lie algebras, representations, angular momentum, spin, and the hydrogen atom, including the hidden symmetry of the Coulomb problem.

Potential use for `pauli`:

- rotation representations before coordinate formulas;
- angular momentum as representation theory rather than a table of quantum-number rules;
- hydrogen and its larger symmetry;
- mathematically explicit treatment of the operator framework.
