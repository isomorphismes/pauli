# Klaas Landsman — *Foundations of Quantum Theory: From Classical Concepts to Operator Algebras*

Open-access Springer book:

https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-319-51777-3

The book explicitly develops quantum theory through its relation to classical physics.

Its contents include finite and general phase spaces, finite-dimensional and general Hilbert spaces, symmetry in quantum mechanics, classical models of quantum mechanics, classical limits, algebraic quantum theory, spontaneous symmetry breaking, measurement, and quantum logic/topos material.

## Why the machine suggested it

This is useful when `pauli` needs to know whether a concept belongs specifically to wave mechanics or has a more general formulation.

Examples:

- state spaces;
- observables;
- symmetry;
- classical limits;
- operator algebras;
- spontaneous symmetry breaking.

The book is also useful for resisting an accidental identification of "quantum mechanics" with one coordinate wavefunction formalism.

## How to use it

The book is broad. Do not drag its entire foundational program into a hydrogen visualizer.

Consult it when a structural claim needs a larger setting—for example, when distinguishing Hilbert-space language from algebraic language or when asking what survives beyond the single-particle Schrödinger picture.
