# Leslie E. Ballentine — *Quantum Mechanics: A Modern Development*

World Scientific publishes the book as a broad treatment of quantum mechanics for advanced students.

Publisher listing:

https://www.worldscientific.com/

## Why the machine suggested it

Ballentine is here as a physicist-side counterweight to the mathematically oriented references.

The attraction is the combination of substantial working quantum mechanics with sustained attention to interpretation and foundations. That can help `pauli` avoid two failures:

- a mathematically clean description with no feeling for what experimental or physical question is being answered;
- a calculation-heavy physics description that substitutes technique for explanation.

## What to consult it for

Potentially useful topics include:

- how standard quantum states and observables are interpreted in actual physics practice;
- measurement and probability;
- scattering and bound-state calculations;
- the relation between formal structure and physical predictions.

When a mathematical account has become so abstract that the physical question has disappeared, a book like this is useful as a reality check.
