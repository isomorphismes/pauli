# Leon A. Takhtajan — *Quantum Mechanics for Mathematicians*

American Mathematical Society, Graduate Studies in Mathematics, volume 95, 2008.

AMS listing:

https://www.ams.org/books/gsm/year/2008/

## Why the machine suggested it

This is a substantial mathematical treatment rather than a short physics problem book. It is useful as a place to check statements that ordinary physics notation often leaves formal:

- Hilbert-space structure;
- operators and spectra;
- canonical quantum mechanics;
- symmetry and mathematical formulations of dynamics;
- the relation between classical structures and quantum ones.

The main reason to keep it near `pauli` is not to make the exposition heavier. It is to have somewhere to check whether an apparently innocent physics sentence has hidden hypotheses.

Examples:

- Is the operator merely symmetric, or actually self-adjoint?
- What is its domain?
- Is an "eigenfunction" really in the Hilbert space?
- Is a formal commutator identity legitimate on the states being discussed?

## Use in this repository

Consult Takhtajan when rigor changes the meaning of the statement.

Do **not** import extra formalism merely because the book contains it. The house style remains: explain the physical/mathematical question first, then introduce whatever machinery is necessary to answer it correctly.
