# V. S. Varadarajan — *Geometry of Quantum Theory*

Suggested because it treats quantum theory through geometry, group actions, kinematics, and structural foundations.

Potential use for `pauli`:

- understanding quantum states without reducing them immediately to coordinate wavefunctions;
- group actions and systems of imprimitivity;
- distinguishing intrinsic structures from basis-dependent formulas.
