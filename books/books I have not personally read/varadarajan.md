# V. S. Varadarajan — *Geometry of Quantum Theory*

Springer page:

https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-0-387-49386-2

The book grew from lectures aimed at mathematically mature readers. Its organization includes projective geometry, quantum logic, Hilbert-space geometry, measure theory on group spaces, systems of imprimitivity, multipliers, kinematics and dynamics, and relativistic free particles.

## Why the machine suggested it

Varadarajan is useful when ordinary wavefunction notation makes the coordinate representation look more fundamental than the state.

The book approaches quantum theory through geometry and group actions. That makes it relevant to questions such as:

- what is intrinsic about a state, apart from a chosen representative vector;
- why phases can be physically redundant while relative phase remains meaningful;
- how symmetries act projectively;
- how kinematics is organized by group actions;
- how position-like observables and symmetry can be related structurally.

## Use in `pauli`

This is not the first source to open for an explicit hydrogen polynomial.

It is a source to open when the exposition risks saying something basis-dependent as if it were intrinsic.

That is exactly the distinction `pauli` is trying to preserve when it puts spherical-harmonic representation spaces before associated-Legendre coordinate formulas.
