# Valter Moretti — *Spectral Theory and Quantum Mechanics*

Suggested primarily as a reference for the operator-theoretic questions that ordinary physics texts often compress too aggressively.

Potential use for `pauli`:

- unbounded operators;
- self-adjointness;
- operator domains;
- spectral theory;
- quantum symmetries.

This is particularly relevant whenever a formal differential expression is being mistaken for a fully specified self-adjoint operator.
