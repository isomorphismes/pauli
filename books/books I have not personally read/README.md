# Books I have not personally read

These are books the machine recommended as potentially useful for `pauli`.

They are **not endorsements by the repository owner**. The owner has not personally read them and is neither approving nor rejecting them.

Each note therefore has a deliberately limited purpose:

- identify what the book is about from publisher/author information and reliable bibliographic descriptions;
- record why it might answer a question that arises in `pauli`;
- identify specific topics worth consulting;
- avoid attributing a style or position to the owner.

## Why keep these

They cover different parts of the same conceptual territory:

- what a quantum state is;
- what an observable is;
- why self-adjointness and spectral theory matter;
- how symmetries act on states;
- how group representations enter mechanics;
- how classical and quantum descriptions are related;
- what "quantization" is supposed to mean;
- how the formalism connects back to physical problems.

No book in this folder should silently become the voice of the repository merely because the machine suggested it.
