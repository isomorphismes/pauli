# Books I have not personally read

These are books the machine recommended as potentially useful for `pauli`.

They are **not endorsements by the repository owner**. They are kept separately because they may be useful references, but the owner has not personally read them and is neither approving nor rejecting them.

## Quantum mechanics and mathematical structure

- [Brian C. Hall — *Quantum Theory for Mathematicians*](hall.md)
- [L. D. Faddeev and O. A. Yakubovskiĭ — *Lectures on Quantum Mechanics for Mathematics Students*](faddeev-yakubovskii.md)
- [Leon A. Takhtajan — *Quantum Mechanics for Mathematicians*](takhtajan.md)
- [George W. Mackey — *Mathematical Foundations of Quantum Mechanics*](mackey.md)
- [V. S. Varadarajan — *Geometry of Quantum Theory*](varadarajan.md)
- [N. P. Landsman — *Foundations of Quantum Theory*](landsman.md)
- [Valter Moretti — *Spectral Theory and Quantum Mechanics*](moretti.md)
- [N. M. J. Woodhouse — *Geometric Quantization*](woodhouse.md)
- [Leslie E. Ballentine — *Quantum Mechanics: A Modern Development*](ballentine.md)

The reason these were suggested is not that they necessarily share one style. They cover different pieces of the conceptual background: symmetry, representation theory, operator theory, spectral theory, geometry, quantization, and foundational structure.
