# Transnational College of LEX — *Who Is Fourier? A Mathematical Adventure*

*Who Is Fourier? A Mathematical Adventure*, written by the Transnational College of LEX and translated by Alan Gleason.

Catalog / borrow record:

https://openlibrary.org/books/OL8526128M/Who_is_Fourier

This belongs in `pauli` for the same reason the drum, flute, oboe, and vowel examples belong here.

The book does not begin by asking the reader to accept “Fourier analysis” as a named subject. It builds toward the idea through waves, sound, pictures, repeated patterns, differentiation, integration, projection, complex numbers, and the human voice.

That makes it a useful style reference for the question currently tracked in issue #11: what ordinary words should we use for the activity usually called harmonic analysis, spectral decomposition, or mode decomposition before attaching one of those technical names?

For this project, the important lesson is:

> let the reader first see a complicated pattern being rebuilt from simpler recurring patterns, then give the standard mathematical name later.

The book is a reference and inspiration, not a source to copy into the public page.
