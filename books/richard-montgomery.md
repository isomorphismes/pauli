# Richard Montgomery

Richard Montgomery is a broad style and geometry reference for `pauli`, not merely a quantum-mechanics reference.

Publication list:

https://people.ucsc.edu/~rmont/papers/list.html

His work ranges across classical mechanics, gauge theory, geometric phases, symplectic and Poisson reduction, control, sub-Riemannian geometry, and the many-body problem. That range is useful here because it prevents gauge theory, symmetry, bundles, and connections from being presented as concepts that belong only to quantum field theory.

## *The Bundle Picture in Mechanics*

Montgomery's 1986 Berkeley PhD thesis:

https://people.ucsc.edu/~rmont/papers/thesis.pdf

This is particularly relevant to the house style. Mechanics can be organized through bundles, connections, reduction, and symmetry rather than treated only as coordinates plus equations of motion.

## Gauge theory outside high-energy physics

Montgomery's work on geometric phase and mechanics is a standing reminder that principal bundles and connections arise in ordinary mechanical systems: orientation changes, shape changes, locomotion, holonomy, and reduction by symmetry.

So `pauli` should never imply:

> gauge theory is a strange formalism invented for quantum field theory.

It is geometry with applications across classical and quantum mechanics.

## Why keep the whole corpus nearby

Montgomery's many-body work, variational methods, Lie-group methods, geometric phases, and sub-Riemannian geometry all support the same habit:

> understand the geometry of the space of possibilities before treating a coordinate calculation as the answer.

That is the reason to link the complete publication list rather than only one supposedly "relevant" paper.
