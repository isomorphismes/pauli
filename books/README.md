# Books

References for the mathematical and expository style of `pauli`.

The common standard is:

> start with the problem, structure, symmetry, or object; explain why it matters; only then introduce coordinates, named special functions, or closed formulas.

The books and authors in the main folder are ones the repository owner has personally asked to keep close. They are not all about hydrogen or quantum mechanics. Some are here because their **style of explanation** is the model: geometry before coordinate tricks, symmetry before labels, and physical meaning before formula manipulation.

## Warning

- [Do not use Jackson as the expository model](jackson-warning.md)

Jackson may be useful for calculations. The warning is about explanatory order, not about the quality of the book.

## House use

A reference can contribute in three different ways:

1. **content** — a theorem, construction, historical fact, or physical derivation;
2. **style** — a way of deciding what to explain first and what can wait;
3. **cross-checking** — a source against which a formula, terminology choice, or historical claim can be checked.

Keep those roles distinct.
