# A. Zee — *Quantum Field Theory in a Nutshell*

Zee is useful as a counterweight to two bad extremes:

- formal mathematics with too little physical motivation;
- cookbook physics where a formula appears, gets manipulated, and disappears without explaining the structure.

The useful balance for `pauli` is:

- say what physical question is being asked;
- keep symmetry and geometry visible;
- calculate enough that the claims are concrete;
- do not confuse computational tricks with explanations;
- allow physical intuition, but tie it to an actual mathematical object.

The project should therefore be willing to say both:

> rotational symmetry tells us to decompose angular states into irreducible rotation representations,

and later:

> here is the explicit associated-Legendre expression needed by the renderer.

The first explains the structure. The second implements it. Neither should masquerade as the other.

Zee is not being treated as the final authority on mathematical rigor. The value here is the willingness to keep physical questions, computation, and structural ideas in the same conversation.
