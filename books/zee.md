# A. Zee — *Quantum Field Theory in a Nutshell*

Zee is useful as a counterweight to both extremes:

- purely formal mathematics with too little physical motivation;
- cookbook physics where a formula appears, gets manipulated, and disappears without explaining the structure.

The useful balance for `laguerre` is:

- say what physical question is being asked;
- keep the symmetry and geometry visible;
- calculate enough that the claims are concrete;
- do not confuse computational tricks with explanations;
- allow intuition, but tie it to an actual mathematical object.

The project should therefore be willing to say both:

> rotational symmetry tells us to decompose into spherical-harmonic representations,

and later:

> here is the explicit polynomial needed by the renderer.

The first explains the structure. The second implements it. Neither should masquerade as the other.
