# Stephanie Frank Singer

Stephanie Frank Singer is an unusually direct reference for `pauli`: her two books put symmetry and representation theory in front of the calculations that they organize.

Author's mathematics and physics page:

https://symmetrysinger.com/index.php?id=math-physics

## *Symmetry in Mechanics: A Gentle, Modern Introduction*

Birkhäuser, 2001.

https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-1-4612-0189-2

The book begins with the two-body problem and develops the modern geometric language only as the mechanics asks for it:

- phase spaces as symplectic manifolds;
- total energy functions as Hamiltonian functions;
- symmetries as Lie group actions;
- infinitesimal symmetries as Lie algebras;
- conserved quantities as momentum maps;
- reduction back into the two-body problem.

That order is worth copying. The named machinery appears because a physical problem has exposed the need for it.

## *Linearity, Symmetry, and Prediction in the Hydrogen Atom*

Springer, 2005.

https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/b136359

This is almost a statement of the central `pauli` project.

Singer develops enough linear algebra, Hilbert-space language, Lie groups, and representation theory to make structural predictions about the hydrogen atom from linearity and symmetry. The book then reaches SO(3), SU(2), SO(4), the hydrogen atom, projective representations, spin, and tensor products.

The important pedagogical fact is that spherical harmonics and orbital labels need not be introduced first as formulas to memorize. They arise inside a representation-theoretic story.

Singer's own description emphasizes that representation theory can predict some of the structure of hydrogen without first solving the Schrödinger equation.

## Standing use in this repository

When `pauli` has a choice between

> here is the formula for an orbital

and

> here is the symmetry, here is the representation it permits, and now here is a concrete realization,

prefer the second order.

Singer is therefore both a content reference and a style reference: build the machinery while moving toward a definite physical question, and make the symmetry do explanatory work.
